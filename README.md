# Epstein Files Archive

A curated collection of publicly released documents related to Jeffrey Epstein, sourced from official government releases and public archives.

## Contents

### Court Documents (`court-documents/`)
**Guiffre v. Maxwell Case (January 2024 Release)**

41 unsealed court documents from Case 1:15-cv-07433-LAP, U.S. District Court, Southern District of New York. These documents were publicly released on January 3, 2024.

Files are named following the court filing convention (e.g., `1320-1.pdf`, `1320-2.pdf`, etc.)

**Source:** [Internet Archive - January 3, 2024 Unsealed Documents](https://archive.org/details/final-epstein-documents)

### DOJ Records (`doj-records/`)
**DOJ Phase 1 Declassified Files (February 2025)**

Declassified documents released by the U.S. Department of Justice on February 27, 2025, under Attorney General Pamela Bondi. This first phase includes:

- Flight logs
- Contact books (redacted)
- Other previously leaked documents formally released by the government

## December 2025 DOJ Release

On December 19, 2025, the DOJ began releasing additional files pursuant to the Epstein Files Transparency Act (H.R.4405). This release includes:
- Thousands of pages of documents
- Ghislaine Maxwell interview transcripts
- Prison surveillance video footage
- Audio recordings
- Grand jury materials (partially redacted)

These files are being released in phases and can be accessed at:
- [DOJ Epstein Library](https://www.justice.gov/epstein/doj-disclosures)
